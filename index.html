👥 CREW ROLES — ONE PER PLAYER

🧑‍🚀1 player — Commander: flies every game.
👥2 players: Science & Comms (collect, identify spectra, send, decode) · Power & Defense (balance, routing, meteors, repairs).
👥3 players: Science Officer · Comms Officer · Systems Engineer (balance, routing, meteors, repairs).
👥4 players: Science Officer · Comms Officer · Power Engineer (balance, routing, repairs) · Defense Gunner (meteors).
🔁Tap a callsign on the first screen to take a role — it swaps with whoever had it. Each month the game goes to the role that owns it.

🎮 THE MINIGAMES

✦Collect Data (Science) — move the craft with ← → or drag and catch falling particles. Gold ones are worth 3.
🔬Spectrum ID (Science) — match the sample's glowing spectral lines to the right element. 3 samples; correct and fast earns more science.
📡Send Data (Comms) — aim and fire packets at Earth's moving dish. A green LOCK ring means a sure hit.
📶Signal Decode (Comms) — watch the colour pattern, then repeat it. 3 rounds, longer each time. Clean decodes upload your data buffer.
⚖️Balance Power (Power) — hold ← → to keep the power beam inside the green zone.
🔌Power Routing (Power) — rotate scrambled conduit tiles to connect the solar feed to the battery. Two puzzles, faster solves give more power.
☄️Save the Craft (Defense) — the laser auto-fires; just aim it at meteors. Hits scorch the hull and can break systems.
🔧Repairs (Power / Systems) — pay to fix broken systems or fly degraded.
